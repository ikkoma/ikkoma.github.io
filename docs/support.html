Which iPhones can draw panels?

iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and newer, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings, Apple Intelligence & Siri. On other iPhones, Ikkoma opens in manual mode: you can build comics from your own photos and drawings and add lettering.

The art engine download

The first time you open Ikkoma, it downloads its art engine from the App Store (a few gigabytes). Keep the app open on Wi-Fi until it finishes. To download over mobile data, turn on Profile, AI model, Download over mobile data. If the download stops, open the app again and it carries on.

A panel looks wrong

Touch and hold the panel, then tap Redraw for a new picture, or Reframe to move and zoom the picture inside the panel. Speech bubbles are added by you, so you can always edit the words.

Something inappropriate appeared

Touch and hold the panel and tap Report image. The panel is hidden straight away. If you want, email us what happened so we can improve the filter.

Characters from photos

Ikkoma describes your photo in words, then deletes the photo. You can edit the description chips before saving. You need to confirm you have permission to use the photo. Famous people can’t be added.

Restore your purchase

Open Profile and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Ikkoma Lifetime. Family Sharing members get it too.

The “Made with Ikkoma” mark

Comics made on the free plan show a small mark when you save or share them. Ikkoma Lifetime removes it from every comic, including ones you already made.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments. You can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your data

Delete comics and characters inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Ikkoma has no account or server, so there is nothing stored anywhere else. See the Privacy Policy.
