Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Ikkoma collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your stories, characters, photos and comics stay on your iPhone.

Who we are

Ikkoma is an iPhone app that turns a short story idea into a comic. In this policy, “Ikkoma”, “we” and “us” mean the developer of the app. You can reach us at ikkoma001@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you make in Ikkoma is stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage:

  • your story ideas, scripts and lettering,
  • your characters and their descriptions,
  • the panels and comics the app draws,
  • your settings.

We have no server, so we cannot see, copy or recover any of it. If you delete the app, all of it is deleted.

How the AI works

  • Script. Apple Intelligence writes your script on your iPhone, using Apple’s on-device Foundation Models.
  • Panels. Ikkoma’s art engine draws each panel on your iPhone. Your story is never sent to us or to any AI company.
  • Apple Image Playground (optional). If you choose to draw a panel with Apple Image Playground, Apple’s own system sheet handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you pick a style there that uses ChatGPT, Apple asks you first, and Apple’s and OpenAI’s terms apply to that request.

Photos you use for characters

You can make a character from a photo. You choose the photo with the iOS photo picker, so Ikkoma only ever receives the one photo you pick, not your library. Apple Intelligence on your iPhone describes the photo in words (for example “short red hair, freckles, green hoodie”). Ikkoma keeps only those words and deletes the photo straight away. The photo is never uploaded, and no face data is stored.

Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have given you permission. The app asks you to confirm this before it saves the character.

Downloads

The art engine and any extra art styles are downloaded from Apple’s App Store servers, the same way the app itself is. We do not run any download server and receive no information about your downloads.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether the unlock is owned, so it can turn on the paid features.

Reporting an image

If a panel looks wrong or inappropriate, you can hold the panel and tap Report image. The panel is hidden on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to email us about it, and then we receive only what you put in that email.

Crash reports

Ikkoma contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Emails to support

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not add you to a mailing list and we never sell or share your email. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Children

Ikkoma is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Delete a comic or character inside the app at any time.
  • Remove the art engine in Profile, AI model, Delete art engine.
  • Delete the app to remove everything Ikkoma stored on your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Ikkoma handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.

Contact
