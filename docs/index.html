Questions

Which iPhones can run Ikkoma?

iPhone 15 Pro and newer with Apple Intelligence turned on. These iPhones can draw panels on the device. On other iPhones you can still make comics from your own photos and drawings and add lettering.

Do I need the internet?

Only once, to download the art engine (a few gigabytes, over Wi-Fi). After that, Ikkoma writes and draws without a connection.

Who owns the comics I make?

You do. Your comics stay on your iPhone, and you can open and export them any time, even on the free plan.

Can I put my friends in a comic?

Yes, if they say it’s OK. Ikkoma asks you to confirm you have permission before saving a character made from a photo. Famous people can’t be added.
